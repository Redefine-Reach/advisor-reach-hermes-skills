Why It Matters

The Right Buyer. The Right Price. The Right Time.

Every day a home sits on the market costs the seller money — in carrying costs, in buyer skepticism, and in eventual price reductions. The goal is never to sell faster at the expense of price. The goal is to sell at peak value by arriving at the market with a buyer already identified — so the launch creates urgency, not anxiety.

"The list price is not a wish. It is a strategy. And strategy — not hope — is what gets you to closing."
