Hermes Skills
The public, portable SKILL.md-format skills a customer's Hermes agent loads to operate AdvisorReach software — this repo, advisor-reach-hermes-skills (browser-use, circle-member-token, computer-use, connect-app, connect-mcp, email-outreach, ffmpeg-skill, gbp-scorecard, listing-presentation, mab-design-iteration, present-file, publish-site, retirement-calculator, video). Grounded in this repo's README.md ("Public, portable skills in the open SKILL.md format — usable by Hermes, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and any SKILL.md-compatible agent").
Circle Member Token
The one-hour Circle Member API access_token that a box mints for one of its own declared Circle members (POST {ADVISORREACH_API_URL}/circle/v1/auth-token with the member's email) and then presents as Authorization: Bearer to https://app.circle.so/api/headless/v1/... to read, find, and comment as that person. Minted on demand by the circle-member-token skill, never stored. Grounded in skills/circle-member-token/SKILL.md ("Getting a token", "Finding a post", "Commenting on a post").
